Can you already sense an answer to the question "Why we Play"? it is Wundt and Lessing's ideas that are seen through this in Skate: "Pleasure in vigorous activity" and "Joy in "being a cause" ". That joy is described as "pleasure in triumphing over obstales, the joy of success, victory". To add on; "Lessing thought it gave the child a deeper sense of his own reality" (note 1).

Gameplay:


Your character is completely customizable; skin, hair style, eye shape, even down to the voice! However a gamer wants to design their avatar- in Skate, desired specifics are possible.
As soon as the character is complete the player will need to get comfortable with the world around them. Parkour is a part of this game, and climbing is encouraged! So, to reach your initial skateboard, the player will need to move in order to access it, then the skating begins :)



Ultamitely, this game is about learning different skate tricks, getting comfortable with using controls to skate, and the pleasure of failure and success! As you continue playing, you will get familiar with the setting "SanVan". What is fun about this game is that it is multiplayer, so players can skate with random players & friends. After some time playing, one may notice that they are getting a sense of skate culture and what that looks like- this game truly is a world of immersion.

I find this game very sentimental, and comforting. I was in the third grade, and all summer long my brother and family friend became familiar with Skate, enjoying watching one another play, fail, laugh, and learn sick tricks. The game Skate is important because it genuinely teaches about skating. Immersing players into the culture of the sport while finding community and learning to embrace failure.
